HIPSTER BACKTEST, PRE-REGISTRATION. Written 17 Oct 2026 by Claude, before any held-out number was computed.
Nothing below changes after the first run. A change means a new file with a new date, and both results get reported.

QUESTION
Can Hipster call an artist's rise before it happens, better than plain baselines? And does the Qloo taste graph add anything beyond momentum?

UNIVERSE
Every artist in the recorded Qloo similar-artist calls (../live/slice1-core-20261015.json, 55 calls, 976 artists).
Kept: artists with a Wikidata id in Qloo's external ids and an English Wikipedia article. 676 titles at fetch time.

PAIRING SOURCE (the truth label)
English Wikipedia daily pageviews, agent=user, all-access, 1 Jul 2023 to 11 Oct 2026 (fetch.py).
Daily views are summed into Monday to Sunday weeks. w(t) is the total for week t.

AT EACH CUTOFF WEEK t (features only use weeks up to and including t)
past(i) = median of w over weeks t-7 to t.
Eligible: past(i) >= 700 (about 100 views a day), and all 16 weeks t-7 to t+8 present.
Breakout(i, t): median of w over weeks t+1 to t+8 >= 1.5 x past(i). This is the event Hipster tries to call.

ARMS (each picks k = 10 eligible artists per cutoff)
R random: expected precision = the breakout base rate among eligible artists that week.
P popularity: the 10 highest past(i).
M momentum (no Qloo): m(i) = ln(1 + mean of w over weeks t-1, t) minus ln(1 + median of w over weeks t-7 to t-2). Top 10 by m.
Q Hipster (Qloo taste graph): s(i) = m(i) + 1.0 x mean of m(j) over Qloo neighbours j of i that are eligible at t.
  Neighbours: artists that share a Qloo similar-artist list with i. No eligible neighbour: the term is 0.
Ties broken by name, so every run is deterministic.

SPLIT
Dev cutoffs: weeks ending 7 Jan 2024 to 29 Dec 2024. Used only to check the code. No parameter is tuned on them.
Held-out cutoffs: weeks ending 5 Jan 2025 to 16 Aug 2026 (the last week whose 8 future weeks end by 11 Oct 2026).

METRIC
Precision at 10 per cutoff, averaged over the held-out cutoffs.
95% interval: moving-block bootstrap over cutoffs, block 8 weeks, 2000 resamples, seed 7, on each arm and on the differences Q minus P, Q minus M, Q minus base rate, M minus P.
Reported too: lift = precision / base rate, and for Q hits the median number of weeks from the cutoff to the peak week.

KILL LINES (decided now)
K1. Hipster is a result only if Q beats P and the base rate on held-out weeks with the lower end of both difference intervals above 0. Otherwise Hipster is a toy, and the handover says so.
K2. Qloo earns its place only if the lower end of the Q minus M interval is above 0. Otherwise the handover says: momentum on Wikipedia does the work and the Qloo graph is decoration in this test.

KNOWN LIMITS (stated before the run)
L1. Leakage: the Qloo calls were made on 15 Oct 2026. The neighbour lists already reflect 2024 to 2026 tastes. Qloo is used only for the universe and the neighbour graph; every time-varying number comes from Wikipedia and is past-only at each cutoff. The graph itself can still carry hindsight.
L2. Wikipedia views measure attention, not listening or sales. A death, a scandal or a tour also counts as a breakout.
L3. The universe comes from one festival lineup (Way Out West 2026) and its Qloo neighbours. It is not all music.
L4. Overlapping 8-week windows make weeks correlated. The block bootstrap is there for that; the intervals may still be too narrow.
